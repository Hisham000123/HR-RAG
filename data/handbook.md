## 1. Introduction

Welcome to NovaRetail Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

This handbook explains the company's internal HR policies, employee benefits, workplace expectations, and administrative procedures.

### Purpose

The purpose of this handbook is to:

* Provide employees with clear HR policy information.
* Establish consistent workplace guidelines.
* Explain employee rights and responsibilities.
* Help employees understand company procedures.

### Scope

This handbook applies to all full-time and part-time employees of NovaRetail Technologies Pvt. Ltd., unless a specific policy states otherwise.

**Important:** This is a fictional sample handbook created for demonstration and software development purposes. It is not an official company policy or legal document.

## 2. Working Hours and Attendance

### 2.1 Standard Working Hours

* Standard working days: Monday to Friday.
* Standard working hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
* Lunch break: 1 hour.
* Weekly working hours: 40 hours, excluding lunch breaks.

Working hours may vary depending on the employee's department, employment agreement, or approved work arrangement.

### 2.2 Attendance

Employees must record their attendance using the company's designated attendance management system.

Employees should notify their reporting manager if they expect to arrive late or cannot attend work.

### 2.3 Overtime

Overtime must be approved by the reporting manager before it is performed.

Eligibility for overtime compensation or compensatory leave depends on the employee's role, employment agreement, and applicable law.

### 2.4 Public Holidays

The company publishes an annual holiday calendar.

Employees should consult the official HR portal for the current list of public holidays applicable to their location.

## 3. Leave Policy

### 3.1 Annual Leave

For this sample policy, eligible full-time employees receive 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave must be requested through the HR management system and approved by the reporting manager.

### 3.2 Sick Leave

Eligible employees receive 12 days of sick leave per calendar year under this sample policy.

Employees should notify their reporting manager as soon as possible when illness prevents them from working.

HR may request supporting documentation where permitted by applicable law and company policy.

### 3.3 Casual Leave

Employees may use up to 6 days of casual leave per calendar year for personal matters, subject to eligibility and approval.

### 3.4 Leave Application Procedure

Employees should:

1. Log in to the HR portal.
2. Select the appropriate leave category.
3. Enter the requested dates and reason, where required.
4. Submit the leave request.
5. Wait for approval from the authorized manager.

### 3.5 Emergency Leave

Employees who experience an emergency should inform their reporting manager as soon as reasonably possible.

HR will review the circumstances and advise the employee on the applicable leave category.

### 3.6 Leave Carry-Forward

Unused annual leave may be carried forward only according to the company's approved leave rules and applicable law.

The exact carry-forward limit should be confirmed with HR.

## 4. Remote Work Policy

### 4.1 Eligibility

Remote work is subject to role requirements, department guidelines, and manager approval.

Not all positions are eligible for remote work.

### 4.2 Remote Work Expectations

Employees working remotely must:

* Remain available during agreed working hours.
* Attend required meetings.
* Protect confidential company information.
* Use approved devices and applications.
* Meet their assigned responsibilities and deadlines.

### 4.3 Remote Work Requests

Employees should submit remote work requests through the designated HR portal or to their reporting manager.

Approval must be obtained before a new or extended remote work arrangement begins.

### 4.4 Equipment and Expenses

Company equipment may be provided according to role requirements and availability.

Expense reimbursement is subject to the company's approved expense policy.

## 5. Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to behave professionally and respectfully.

### 5.1 Professional Behavior

Employees must:

* Treat colleagues, customers, and business partners respectfully.
* Communicate professionally.
* Avoid discriminatory or abusive behavior.
* Protect company property and resources.
* Follow applicable company policies.

### 5.2 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest to HR or their designated manager.

Examples include outside business activities that interfere with job responsibilities or personal interests that could improperly influence business decisions.

### 5.3 Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential information belonging to the company, its employees, customers, and business partners.

Confidential information must not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals.

### 5.4 Disciplinary Action

Policy violations may result in corrective or disciplinary action, depending on the circumstances, the severity of the violation, and applicable law.

## 7. Performance Reviews

### 7.1 Review Frequency

For this sample policy, formal performance reviews are conducted twice a year.

Review schedules may vary by department.

### 7.2 Review Criteria

Performance reviews may consider:

* Achievement of individual goals.
* Quality of work.
* Technical and professional skills.
* Communication and collaboration.
* Reliability and accountability.
* Contribution to team objectives.

### 7.3 Performance Improvement Plans

When performance concerns are identified, a manager may establish a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

A PIP may specify:

* Areas requiring improvement.
* Expected performance standards.
* Support and resources provided.
* Review milestones.
* The period allowed for improvement.

Employees should contact HR if they have questions about the review process.

## 8. Compensation and Payroll

### 8.1 Payroll Schedule

For this fictional example, employee salaries are paid monthly, generally on the final working day of the month.

The actual payroll date may change because of banking schedules, holidays, or operational requirements.

### 8.2 Payslips

Employees can access payslips through the designated payroll portal.

Payslips may include salary components, deductions, and other payroll information.

### 8.3 Salary Queries

Employees who identify a potential payroll error should contact the payroll or HR team.

They should provide the relevant pay period and a description of the discrepancy through an approved secure channel.

### 8.4 Salary Confidentiality

Employees should handle payroll information responsibly.

## 9. IT and Data Security

### 9.1 Acceptable Use

Company systems must be used responsibly and in accordance with applicable IT policies.

Employees must not:

* Share passwords or authentication codes.
* Install unauthorized software on managed devices.
* Bypass security controls.
* Access information without authorization.
* Use company systems for unlawful activities.

### 9.2 Password Security

Employees should use strong, unique passwords and enable multifactor authentication where available.

Suspected credential compromise must be reported promptly to the IT or security team.

### 9.3 Data Protection

Employees must handle personal and confidential information according to company security procedures and applicable privacy laws.

Sensitive information must be stored and transmitted using approved systems.

### 9.4 Security Incidents

Employees should immediately report suspected phishing, malware, unauthorized access, or data exposure to the designated security team.

Employees should not investigate suspected incidents beyond their authorization.

## 10. Anti-Harassment Policy

NovaRetail aims to maintain a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation.

### 10.1 Prohibited Conduct

Prohibited conduct may include:

* Unwanted verbal or physical behavior.
* Sexual harassment.
* Discrimination based on legally protected characteristics.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Retaliation against individuals who raise concerns in good faith.

### 10.2 Reporting Concerns

Employees may report concerns to:

* The HR department.
* Their reporting manager, if appropriate.
* The designated ethics or complaints officer.
* Another reporting channel specified by company policy.

Employees may bypass their reporting manager if that person is involved in the concern.

### 10.3 Investigation

Reports will be reviewed through an appropriate process.

The company will seek to handle complaints sensitively and restrict access to information where appropriate.

Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance because an investigation may require information to be shared with relevant individuals.

### 10.4 Retaliation

Retaliation against an individual for making a good-faith complaint or participating in an investigation is prohibited.

### 10.5 India-Specific Requirements

For workplaces in India, sexual harassment complaints must be handled in accordance with applicable law, including the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013, where applicable.

The company's designated Internal Committee and reporting procedures should be documented in the official policy.

## 11. Resignation and Exit Process

### 11.1 Resignation

Employees intending to resign should submit written notice through the designated HR process.

The applicable notice period is determined by the employment agreement and relevant company policies, subject to applicable law.

### 11.2 Handover

Before their final working day, employees are expected to:

* Document ongoing responsibilities.
* Transfer relevant work to designated colleagues.
* Return company equipment.
* Complete required exit formalities.
* Follow instructions for transferring or closing access to company systems.

### 11.3 Final Settlement

HR and payroll will process eligible final payments and deductions according to applicable law, company policy, and the employee's contractual terms.

The employee should contact HR for information about the expected timeline and required documents.

### 11.4 Experience and Relieving Letters

Eligible former employees may request employment-related documents through the designated HR channel.

Issuance and timelines are subject to applicable law and company procedures.
